# Vision 001: Cute Demo. Where's the Rocket Ship?

**From:** Chad "Chaz" Brightwell, Founder & Chief Vibes Officer, MemeCorp
**To:** The Meme-Tinder team
**Priority:** P0 (obviously)

---

🚀 Team! Welcome aboard.

I spent the whole weekend with the Meme-Tinder demo your predecessors left behind. I swiped. I laughed. I showed my mum. She swiped right on "It works on my machine" and said, and I quote, "this is very relatable."

So the good news: **the vibes are there.**

The bad news: it is a toy. Last night I restarted the thing, and **every single like I had was gone.** Gone! My mum's favourites too! I have never felt so betrayed by software. A rocket ship does not forget its passengers.

I don't know how any of this works under the hood, and I don't want to. I know *what* I want it to feel like: you open it, you swipe, you laugh, you can't stop, and **nothing you love ever disappears.**

So here are my five big bets. They are all equally important, which means they are all P0, which means I need you to do them all. 😎

---

## Big Bet 1: Nothing You Love Ever Disappears 💾

If I like a meme today, it must still be liked tomorrow. Restart the app, restart the server, restart my mum's laptop. I don't care. My memes stay my memes.

## Big Bet 2: Post Your Own Meme 📤

Right now the app has 30 memes and then it's over. That's not a swipe economy, that's a short film. Let **anyone add their own memes** to the pile. More memes means more swiping means more growth.

Keep it simple. Pick an image, give it a funny caption, post. I saw our current memes are lovely little vector things (the ones that stay sharp when you zoom), so let's keep that vibe. People should be able to post those too.

Everyone's new meme should show up in other people's piles, not only their own.

## Big Bet 3: Hot or Not 🔥

I want to know which memes are winning. I want **numbers**.

- On every meme, I want to see how much people love it. Something like "87% of people loved this".
- I want a **Trending** spot: the memes people are loving right now. Something I can screenshot and put on LinkedIn.
- And a counter that shows **memes per minute**. How fast are people swiping, right now, across the whole app? This is my north star metric. I want to watch it go up.

## Big Bet 4: Feel the Swipe 💥

Right now swiping works, but it doesn't *feel* like anything. Let's make it a moment.

- When I swipe right, I want to see a big **LIKE** stamp. Left, a **NOPE** stamp. Like a real dating app.
- The next card should arrive with some energy. Nothing boring.
- Surprise me. A little confetti on a streak? A special moment on your 100th meme? I trust you. I have good taste but no skills.

## Big Bet 5: Guardrails 🛡️

My friend who's a CTO says that if you're using robots to build the product (that's you, I assume, with the agents), you need "guardrails" so nothing sneaks in that breaks the app. I don't know what that means, but I want it. **Every change should be checked automatically before it goes in**, and the code should look consistent, like one person wrote it, even though it's a whole team.

---

## What "done" looks like

I can open Meme-Tinder on my phone, swipe ten memes, restart everything, and come back to find all my likes still there. I can post a meme, and my mum can see it on her phone. I can show the Trending list to a friend and say "see, they love it."

## A few things I haven't thought through

I'll be honest, I haven't thought about everything:

- What happens if two people post the same meme?
- What happens if somebody posts something... not nice?
- Is "Trending" the last hour, or all time? Surprise me. I know it when I see it.

If you have questions, ask me. If I don't answer in time, use your judgement. I trust you. (I trust you *a lot*. That's why this is all P0.)

## Not now

Money, ads, premium tiers, and anything I haven't mentioned. We'll get there once we're a rocket ship.

Let's go. 🚀

- Chaz
